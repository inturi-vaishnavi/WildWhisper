A quieter kind of technology

Curiosity, close to home.

WildWhisper is a local-first AI naturalist for exploring the world around you. Your image understanding and optional voice transcription happen on your computer, keeping the experience grounded and private by design.

When enabled, ElevenLabs sends answer text online for speech generation, and Sentry sends monitoring and performance telemetry. WildWhisper is offline-capable, not completely offline when these services are used.
